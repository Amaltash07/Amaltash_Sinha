02 / THE DESIGNER

✳

Designing the experience,
not just the space.

I’m a game designer focused on how players read, explore, and interact with a game world.

I work across level design, gameplay systems, world design, and narrative. I’m interested in the relationship between mechanics and space: how a route teaches, how an encounter creates tension, and how an environment can tell a story without spelling everything out.
